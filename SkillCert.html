Purdue University

Python Programming

During my Masters of Science in Mechanical Engineering, I learned how to program in Pythong through the Every Boiler Engineer Codes (EBEC) course. Due to the accessibility and versatility of Python in industry and research today, it was a valuable skill to use for analyzing data, developing tools for my own research, and developing personal programming projects.

This certification highlights my ability to use Python in small problems that require a specialized tool to solve, such as identifying the impingement point of supersonic shock. Additionally, the lesson in creating functions streamlined the development of larger, more efficient programs to tackle more involved use-cases, such as 3D model generation for my thesis work.

Southwestern Illinois College

Multiposition Welding

While completing my Bachelors of Science in Mechanical Engineering, I sought to continue my hands-on skill development by completing a welding certificate through SWIC. I learned how to weld using stick and MIG equipment during my Associates in Applied Science in Machine Drafting, and the final course I took taught me how to use these techniques in other orientations, or positions.

My initial welding experience was in flat and horizontal welding, with the second course taught vertical and overhead welding using stick and then MIG. By learning to weld and receiving this certificate, I have a better understanding of what makes a quality weld and when designs should use welding or traditional fasteners for strength or ease of disassembly.

American Design Drafting Association

Certified Machine Drafter

During my Associates in Applied Science in Machine Drafting, I took the American Deisgn Drafting Association's exam for Certified Machine Drafters. It assessed knowledge of technical drawing across subjects of specialized symbols, dimensioning and tolerancing, and other drafting content.

My time at SWIC learning hand-drafting, 2D CAD, and 3D modeling, as well as hands-on skills such as maching and welding, allowed me to cement my knowledge of the process from first conceptualizing to fabrication. This full scope understanding of how drafting and the details experessed through accurate notation and GD&T allowed me to achieve a certification that illustrates my competency in creating detailed drawings for mechanical parts.

College Reading and Learning Association
